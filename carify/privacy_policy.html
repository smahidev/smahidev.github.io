1. Introduction

Welcome to Carify ("we," "our," or "us"). Carify v2.0 is a cloud-based fleet and personal vehicle management application created by DEVSMAHI. It helps individuals manage their own Personal Garage (personal and family cars) and helps business owners, fleet managers, and drivers organize company vehicles, record fuel and itemized maintenance expenses, monitor upcoming renewals, store digital vehicle papers in a Document Vault, coordinate on-demand location checks, and communicate with their team across workspaces.

Our Privacy Commitment: Every Personal Garage and Company Workspace operates inside its own private, isolated environment. You can manage a Personal Garage and optionally participate in a Company Workspace from one account and switch cleanly between them. We never sell your personal information or fleet records to third-party data brokers, and you can permanently delete your account inside the app at any time.

2. Information We Collect

A. Account & Profile Information

  • Google Sign-In Details: Your name, email address, and optional profile photo provided when you sign in with your Google account.
  • Multi-Workspace Profile & Team Details: Contact information you enter (phone number and address), your Personal Garage and optional Work workspace memberships, roles (workspace owner, administrator, or driver), approval status, rotatable 6-character invite codes, and offline driver profiles created by administrators (which may optionally be linked when a driver later joins via an invite code).
  • App Preferences: Your chosen language (English, French, or Arabic), display currency, and light, dark, or system appearance preference.

B. Fleet, Expense, Document & Location Data

  • Vehicle Details: Vehicle name, license plate, Vehicle Identification Number (VIN), brand, model, year, fuel type, transmission, odometer readings, assigned driver, custom maintenance service intervals (oil change, timing belt, tires, battery), and renewal dates for insurance, technical inspection, and road tax.
  • Fuel & Expense Records: Expense records across 14 categories entered either as a direct total or itemized line items (such as fuel, oil changes, repairs, tires, tolls, insurance, inspections, road tax, and fines), including currency, date, odometer reading, optional fuel volume in liters, notes, and uploaded receipt photos.
  • Vehicle Documents (Document Vault): Digital photos, scans, or PDF files of vehicle registration papers, insurance policies, technical inspection certificates, and road tax receipts uploaded by administrators or assigned drivers for roadside access.
  • Team Messages: Text messages, sender names, timestamps, and optional photos or receipts shared with members of your workspace.
  • On-Demand Location Sharing (Foreground Only): Carify does not track your location in the background or continuously. Precise GPS coordinates and accuracy are captured only when a workspace administrator sends a single location request (which expires automatically after 15 minutes if unanswered) and the assigned driver explicitly taps to share their current position once while using the app. Drivers can decline any request at any time.

C. Technical, Diagnostic & Advertising Information

  • App Reliability & Diagnostics: Anonymous crash reports, device model, operating system version, and general usage statistics used to fix bugs and improve speed.
  • Push Notification Delivery: A device messaging identifier used solely to deliver alerts for maintenance reminders, approvals, location requests, and team messages.
  • Advertising Identifiers: Standard device advertising identifiers and approximate regional signals processed to show limited, frequency-capped ads.

4. Cloud Storage, Security & Retention

  • Secure European Union Hosting: Your synced Personal Garage and Company Workspaces, vehicles, expenses, Document Vault files, messages, and shared location responses are stored on encrypted cloud servers located in the European Union.
  • Encryption & Workspace Isolation: All communications between the app and our servers are protected with industry-standard HTTPS encryption, and strict access controls keep each Personal Garage and Company Workspace separate and private.
  • Automatic Data Retention: To minimize stored data, in-app notifications automatically expire and are removed after 90 days, and on-demand location requests (including any shared coordinates) automatically expire and are removed after 7 days (pending unanswered requests expire in 15 minutes).
  • Offline Resilience: Recent records and documents are securely cached on your device so drivers and managers can continue viewing and logging entries in low-signal areas; changes synchronize automatically once internet access returns.
  • Upgrading from Earlier Versions: Users upgrading from Carify v1 can optionally transfer their previously saved records from their device into their cloud workspace.

7. Account & Data Deletion

How to Permanently Delete Your Carify Account & Data

Method 1 — Instant In-App Deletion (Recommended):

  1. Open Carify on your device while signed in.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) icon at the top of the screen.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account (Supprimer le compte / حذف الحساب).
  4. Confirm your choice in the dialog.

What gets deleted immediately: Your sign-in access, personal profile information, uploaded profile photo, notification delivery identifiers, personal notification history, and all workspace memberships are permanently deleted. Any Personal Garage or Company Workspace that you own is also permanently erased along with all of its vehicles, expense records, uploaded receipts, Document Vault files, team messages, invite codes, offline driver profiles, and location responses.

Method 2 — Email Request (If You Uninstalled the App):

Send an email to dev.smahi@gmail.com from your registered Google email address with the subject "Carify Account Deletion Request". Verified requests are completed within 7 business days.

Company Workspace Records: When an individual team member (non-owner) deletes their personal account, shared business records belonging to another owner's Company Workspace (such as company vehicles, historical accounting invoices, and organization vehicle documents) remain accessible to the workspace administrator for operational continuity, and any vehicle linked to that member is unassigned. When a workspace owner deletes their account, their owned workspace and all of its records are permanently removed, while teammates retain their own accounts and Personal Garages.
